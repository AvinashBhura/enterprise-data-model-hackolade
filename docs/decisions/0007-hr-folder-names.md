# D7 — HR folders follow the HR taxonomy

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-12
Approved by: Avinash Bhura, in the [transition strategy document](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/5f38deb5-5ee0-414e-9516-58e23c95726c)

## Context

The LinkML HR folders (`PeopleServices`) do not match the approved HR taxonomy.

## Decision

HR subdomain folders are named after the taxonomy. `PeopleServices` becomes `CoreHR`; `Position` moves to `WorkforcePlanning`, its origin in the taxonomy.

## Consequences

Folder names and model ownership match the taxonomy's single-origin rule.
